Key Design Decisions

  • AvailabilityEntry is per-day × per-court × per-shift — a guard available for morning at Court A and evening at Court B on the same day = two entries
  • isManualOverride flag on ShiftAssignment — lets warnings stay visible even after manual edits, and distinguishes auto vs human assignments in reports
  • FairnessRecord is materialized on publish — not computed on the fly — so historical data survives schema changes and is fast to query for cross-period scoring
  • ScheduleWarning has structured fields (courtId, userId, date) — not just free text — so the UI can highlight specific cells in the schedule grid
  • ShiftCapacityRule.dayOfWeek is nullable — null means "applies to all days", allowing day-specific overrides (e.g. different Friday capacity)
